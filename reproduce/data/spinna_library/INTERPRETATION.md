# Interpretation of the fixed library comparison

The complete precommitted160-field benchmark supports a useful, bounded simulated decision result. Nested flags61/80 inadequate libraries versus49/80 for official SPINNA plus our declared adequacy wrapper. It falsely flags2/80 adequate cases versus1/80. Adequate libraries are not rejected in78/80 versus79/80. This is12 fewer misses for one additional false alert, not uniform superiority.

The difference is concentrated in weak missing-trimer alternatives:10/20 versus1/20 at5% trimer subunits, and11/20 versus8/20 at10%. Both detect20/20 at20% and30%. All controls, intervals, masks and ties/losses remain. Paired correctness is16 nested-only,5 comparator-only,123 both correct and16 both incorrect; no unsupported primary calls.

The primary nested interval was fixed before these fields. Fresh seeds came from a previously studied simulator; this is prospective computational benchmarking, not a new biological preparation or broad external generalization. All160 nested/S4/S3/S2 inferences and stored nested pivot/CDF/hash arrays replay. Official minima, native uncertainty and19-refit rank calculations verify from saved records; the full SPINNA simulations were not rerun on all160 fields.

Native SPINNA20-refit fraction/score uncertainty is retained. The extra full-refit parametric-bootstrap adequacy p-value is OUR approximate fitted-nuisance wrapper, not an official SPINNA test. Its finite19-refit resolution, fitted nuisance parameters and actual false-alert frequencies matter. The directional nested lower-limit rule and omnibus NND-distance rule differ; rates and computation are not matched. Exact binomial intervals in SUMMARY.json describe small40/20-field cohorts, not new400/domain coverage qualification.

For this balanced benchmark only, empirical loss difference is c_false_alert-12*c_miss. Its sign depends on costs; none was chosen to claim success. The descriptive OR check is tabulated with no joint alpha guarantee. No real prevalence, biological cost or decision benefit has been measured.

The tested comparator is one explicit restricted monomer/12nm-dimer fit, with two NND orders and supplied true efficiency/uncertainty. Expanded-library fits, other neighbor orders, density masks and other SPINNA settings were not benchmarked. Nearest-neighbor distributions contain higher-order information; this does not prove exclusive information or general SPINNA inferiority. dTC plus cumulant subtraction accesses the same population contrast.

All eight acquisition contracts remain unqualified for calibrated structural rejection. These simulations evaluate the fixed library and observation model.

Collection took 9,361.685 seconds of wall time. Recomputing the Crosslabel and baseline intervals from saved statistics took 56.358 seconds. The 160 fields, fitted-null simulations, and 40 optimizer calls per field are different units. The measured phases omit some startup, packing, and idle time, so they cannot establish equal computational cost.

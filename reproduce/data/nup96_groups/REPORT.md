# Recorded-group Nup96 audit

One historical four-code Reinhardt et al. acquisition, 1,218 originally
picked groups and 917,449 retained localizations (LP ≤ 8 nm). There were 1,206
groups with the required internal centroid correspondence, and 12 unsupported.
Of the supported groups, 1,205 had a distinct-three-code anchored neighborhood
within 30 nm; 1,198 had one in both temporal halves. One supported group had no
witness. Keeping the two most abundant codes or pooling codes gave zero in
every group, as the construction requires.

The witness changes the recorded-catalogue interpretation: withhold a
two-permanent-code description of those neighborhoods if the codes and
group membership are correct. This is an observed-group calculation; it
does not establish the error rate of inferred groups or the nested spatial
interval on experimental data. The controls test the code requirement
and persistence within one acquisition. They are neither independent
preparations nor experimentally verified monomer/dimer negatives.

Picked pores do not give an unselected full-field observation window. A
consistent centroid match does not establish absence of misassigned or
extraneous records. Localization repeats are retained. Removing or recoloring
226,604 of 911,477 supported records would be the minimum to leave at most
two occupied codes per group, not a minimum for erasing the spatial witness
and not a bound on actual wrong-source events. The source-error budget remains
unknown; no fitted fractions, true molecule counts, structural geometry,
or calibrated biological rejection are reported.
